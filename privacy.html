Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Data Collection

This journal is a minimalist, privacy-first platform. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or sell your analytical data. Any information stored (such as theme preferences) is kept in your browser's local storage.

Local Storage

We use localStorage to persist your session and preferences. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the "Export" functionality in the dashboard.

Contact

For inquiries regarding data handled by this system, please contact the administrator via the dashboard signal portal.
