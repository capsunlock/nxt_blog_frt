This Journal is an exercise in digital restraint. We believe the modern web is too loud, too heavy, and too distracted.

Inspired by the brutalist movement, we prioritize structure over ornamentation. Every pixel should serve a purpose; every line of code should have a reason to exist. We build for clarity, not just for eyes, but for minds.
