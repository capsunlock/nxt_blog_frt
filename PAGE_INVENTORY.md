### Flow 1: Reader Discovers Author
```

    ↓
author_page.html?id=1
    ├─ Bio & avatar
    ├─ All posts by author
    ├─ Series by author
    ├─ Social links
    └─ Subscribe button ✅
```

### Flow 2: Author Resets Password
```
login.html (click "Lost Access?")
    ↓
password_reset.html (stage 1)
    ├─ Enter email ✅
    ↓
password_reset.html (stage 2)
    ├─ Check inbox ✅
    ↓
password_reset.html?token=abc (stage 3)
    ├─ Enter new password ✅
    ↓
password_reset.html (stage 4)
    ├─ Success! ✅
```
